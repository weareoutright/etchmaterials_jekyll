<p>Baltimore, MD – January 22, 2026 – ETCH, Inc. (ETCH), a groundbreaking technology company
that transforms natural gas into clean hydrogen and solid carbon, announced today that it has named
Pankaj Chadha Chief Operating Officer. Mr. Chadha will report to Katie Ellet, CEO, ETCH.</p>

<p>"Pankaj is exactly the right person for ETCH at this moment. He has a long background in hydrogen,
ammonia and carbon; great success in leading multi-site teams and optimizing operations; and
understands how to turn vision into execution," said Katie Ellet, CEO, ETCH. "Pankaj knows
instinctively how to move an organization forward operationally and he works collaboratively to bring
innovative solutions to challenges. We are delighted to welcome him to the ETCH team."</p>

<p>Mr. Chadha joins ETCH as a seasoned operations and business leader with over 35 years of experience
driving transformation in plant operations, environmental health and safety, sustainability initiatives,
and strategic business development. He has a proven track record of delivering measurable results
across diverse industrial sectors including chemicals, manufacturing, and emerging clean energy
technologies.</p>

<p>"Hydrogen and critical materials like carbon underpin major industrial sectors including chemicals,
steel and transportation, while also enabling new high-value technologies and supply chains. ETCH
has an excellent team, unsurpassed technology, and is in a position to contribute positively to the world
and U.S economies," said Mr. Chadha. "We all want to work for companies that are leaders and I was
immediately drawn to ETCH and the work it is doing."</p>

<p>Most recently Mr. Chadha served as Senior Director, Market Strategy &amp; Sustainability at Monolith
since 2022. There he built the decarbonization assessment function and developed comprehensive
market strategies for decarbonized hydrogen, ammonia and carbon black products. Previously he
served as a consultant for Monolith on hydrogen and carbon black strategies. Before that Mr. Chadha
was with SI Group from 2011 to 2019, most recently as Senior Director, Operations &amp; EHS Asia
Pacific, SI Group. Mr. Chadha was Managing Director, EMEA, at Seale and Associates from 2009-
2011. He was a Managing Director at Nitrex from 2004-2008 and Chief Executive, Industrial
Chemicals at Imperial Chemical Industries (ICI India) from 1983 to 2004.</p>

<p>Mr. Chadha received a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology. He
completed Advanced Management Programs at Leeds University, UK and Indian Institute of
Management Ahmedabad, as well as additional specialized training in finance and strategic leadership.</p>

<p style="font-weight:700">About ETCH</p>
<p>
ETCH, Inc. is a revolutionary materials and energy technology company disrupting the natural 
gas paradigm by transforming methane into high-value solid carbon and clean hydrogen without 
the use of water or the generation of process emissions. The ETCH Process™ is engineered
