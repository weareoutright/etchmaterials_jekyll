<p>Baltimore, MD – August 13, 2025 – ETCH, Inc. (ETCH), a revolutionary decarbonization
company producing clean hydrogen and solid carbon from natural gas, announced today
that it has named Cary Cook VP, Business Development. Cook will report to ETCH CEO
Katie Ellet.</p>

<p>
“Cary brings a mix of business development acumen, experience in the space, and hands-
on execution, which is exactly what we need as we move from early traction to real scale.
Having collaborated with him before, I know he will hit the ground running and make an
immediate impact,” said Katie Ellet, CEO, ETCH.</p>

<p>
“What drew me to ETCH is the combination of delivering strong economics, low carbon,
the ability to deploy in virtually any setting, and the production of not just one, but two high
value products,” said Cook. “No one has been able to deliver this caliber solution before
and the prospect of solving these issues is extremely exciting.”</p>

<p>
Before joining ETCH, Cook was Vice President of Business Development and Marketing
for Air Liquide’s North America Hydrogen Mobility business, where he led business growth
initiatives and managed commercial and marketing teams within the hydrogen mobility
sector. Previously, Cook spent several years at SI Group in roles including Global Director
of Market Development, Global Sales Director, and Business Development Director. At SI
Group, he was responsible for the Oilfield business unit&#39;s sales globally, oversaw Industrial
Solutions BU market development, managed international sales teams, and was
responsible for driving strategic business development across multiple product lines.
Earlier in his career, Cook held various positions at Nalco Champion overseeing market
strategy development, customer and account management, and supported customer
solutions in specialty chemicals for the energy sector.</p>

<p>
Cook holds a Bachelor of Business Administration in Marketing from Texas A&amp;M
University.</p>

<p style="font-weight:700">About ETCH</p>
<p>Formulated in the labs of Johns Hopkins University and a recipient of a competitive DOE
ARPA-E grant, ETCH, Inc. is a revolutionary decarbonization company that eliminates
carbon from natural gas to produce both clean hydrogen and solid carbon. No greenhouse
gases are ever produced. The ETCH Process™ will accelerate the clean energy transition
through a solution that delivers unrivaled environmental impact, economic value, and
versatility. For more information on ETCH, please visit www.etchmaterials.com.
</p>
