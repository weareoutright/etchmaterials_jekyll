Collaborate with ETCH

ETCH is seeking an additional 1-2 partners to execute a project with our revolutionary low-carbon hydrogen and solid carbon production technology.
This limited opportunity can offer early adopters a significant competitive advantage to securing reliable, economic gaseous hydrogen to support ongoing operations and decarbonization of assets. The units proposed for these projects will be capable of producing up to 5,000 kgs/day. ETCH has limited the opportunity to ensure strong collaboration and deployment support. With only a few partnership slots open, interested organizations are urged to respond promptly.

Please submit your project by January 31, 2026, to be considered for this opportunity. If you have any questions, use the "Submit Your Project" form below to contact ETCH!

Scope of Collaboration

Partner Benefits Include:

Priority access to our next-generation low-carbon hydrogen.

Customized solution tailored to your operational requirements.

Favorable economic terms as part of this collaboration.

Recognition as a leader in decarbonization technology advancement.

ETCH WILL:

  • Design, install, and operate a 5 metric ton per day hydrogen production facility on site utilizing our proprietary ETCH Process™
  • Own and maintain the equipment
  • Produce hydrogen at agreed upon pressure and purity specifications
  • Handle and remove carbon from site, unless Partner has a need for the carbon, in which case details will be further discussed

PARTNERS WILL:

  • Provide on-site or near-site land suitable for the construction of the facility
  • Collaborate closely on design and engineering to ensure the technology fits your specific needs and purpose
  • Provide comprehensive operational data to support performance evaluation if the ETCH Process™ is integrated into Partner's operations
  • Enter into a supply contract for the duration of the project, with the possibility of extension based on mutual agreement

ETCH, INC. is a revolutionary decarbonization company making clean hydrogen and solid carbon products from natural gas. No greenhouse gases are generated, and no water is used. The ETCH Process™ will accelerate the clean energy transition through a solution that delivers unrivaled economic value, minimal environmental impact, high efficiency, and extreme versatility. The ETCH Process can deliver competitively priced, clean H2 wherever needed and can be implemented at both small scales for distributed production and large scales for centralized production.
